Emotional States

Tap a card to flip it. Every one of these takes estar, because emotions are the E in PLACE. The adjective has to match the person: -o for masculine, -a for feminine, add -s for plural.

Bonus from Feelings: estar harto/a (de) = to be fed up (with); to be sick (of).
Estoy harto de la tarea. (I'm fed up with homework.)

accents on estás, está, estáis, están

The location trap

Event → ser

La fiesta es en mi casa.

Where an event takes place.

Person or thing → estar

Mi casa está en Miami.

Where someone or something is.
